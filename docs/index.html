Pairs trading / statistical arbitrage

cointegration

Visa and Mastercard pass the Engle-Granger cointegration test on a three-year formation window (p = ), so their spread should mean-revert. The hedge ratio () comes only from that window. In the two-year trading window we go long the spread at -2 standard deviations, short at +2, exit at the mean - with costs on every position change. Most candidate pairs (KO/PEP, XOM/CVX, JPM/BAC...) fail the cointegration test outright; that filter is the point.

03

50/200 moving average crossover

backtest

The golden-cross rule on SPY over 15 years: hold while the 50-day average is above the 200-day, otherwise sit in cash. Signals are delayed one day to remove lookahead bias, every switch pays 10 bps, and the benchmark is plain buy-and-hold. The result below is what honest testing usually does to simple strategies.
